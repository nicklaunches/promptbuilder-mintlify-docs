## General

**What is Prompt Builder?**
Prompt Builder helps you create, refine, and reuse prompts across Generator, Optimizer, Tester, and Library.

**What models can I use in Tester?**
Tester uses a curated set of models in the app. Pick the model you want to run your prompt with.

**Can I reuse prompts across modules?**
Yes. Save prompts to My Prompts and reuse them in Generator, Optimizer, and Tester.

## Usage

**How do I start a new Tester chat with a prompt?**
Use Run from Optimizer or Library, or paste a prompt directly into Tester.

**Where are my prompts stored?**
All saved prompts live in My Prompts inside Prompt Library.

## Teams

**Do my team members need their own plan?**
No. Inside a team workspace, everyone uses the owner's plan. Members on the Free plan get the owner's credits as long as they work in that workspace.

**Whose credits does a teammate use?**
The workspace owner's, while they're in the team workspace. In their personal workspace, and in the Chrome extension, they use their own plan. If the owner's plan ends, members go back to their own plan.

**Can I see how many credits each member used?**
Yes. Owners and admins see it in the Members table under **Team members**. See [Team Workspaces](/features/team-workspaces#credits-in-a-team-workspace).

## Troubleshooting

**Why am I seeing an error?**
Check your connection, refresh, and try again. If it persists, contact support with details.
