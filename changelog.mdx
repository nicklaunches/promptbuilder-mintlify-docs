<Update label="Jul 2, 2026">
## Folders & Team Workspaces
Two big library upgrades: organize your prompts into folders, and share a workspace with your team.
- **Library folders**: Group prompts into folders with a folder rail (All prompts, Unfiled, and your folders, each with a count). Create, rename, and delete folders — deleting a folder moves its prompts to Unfiled, never deletes them.
- **Move to folder**: File a prompt from its actions menu, or select several and move them in bulk.
- **Team workspaces**: Create a shared workspace where prompts and folders live together for your whole team.
- **Invite members**: Add teammates by email as **Admins** or **Members**, with pending-invite and seat management from **Team members**.
- **Roles**: Owner (billing + workspace), Admin (people + content), Member (create + edit).
- **Workspace switcher**: Move between your personal library and any team workspace from the sidebar.

Team workspaces are a paid feature. We'd love your feedback at **support@promptbuilder.cc**.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 10, 2026">
## New Prompt Builder
A complete redesign of the prompt building experience, rebuilt for speed.
- **Prompt types**: Pick the right type for your task (Standard, Research, Writing, Planning, Agent, Image, Video, Code, or Automation), or let Auto detect it for you.
- **Instructions**: Save reusable instructions for voice, context, and constraints, applied to every generation.
- **Improve your prompt**: Answer up to 3 clarifying questions and regenerate a sharper prompt.
- **Follow-up prompts**: Get contextual next-step suggestions to explore variations after each build.
- **Additional context**: See the assumptions the engine made for full transparency.
- **Versioning**: Every refine is tracked on a timeline; revert to any earlier version.
- **Faster everywhere**: Prompts are editable the moment the body finishes streaming, with context and suggestions filling in right after.
- The previous version is still available at [legacy.promptbuilder.cc](https://legacy.promptbuilder.cc/).

We'd love your feedback at **support@promptbuilder.cc**.
</Update>
