Instructions are standing directives that Prompt Builder weaves into your prompts automatically. Set them once — "Be concise", "Don't sound like AI", "Always answer first" — and they apply every time you generate or optimize, without retyping.

## Where they apply

Enabled instructions travel with your work in the **Generator**, the **Optimizer**, and the **Tester**. In the composer you'll see an **Instructions** button with a badge showing how many are currently active.

## What an instruction looks like

Each instruction has:

- **Title** — a short name (required), e.g. "Answer first"
- **Detail** — the rule itself (up to 1,000 characters), e.g. "Lead with the answer or bottom line, then add supporting detail."
- **Enabled** — a toggle. Only enabled instructions are applied, and the toggle state syncs everywhere.

## Manage instructions

Open **Instructions** from the sidebar to manage them all on one page:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Create">
    Click **New instruction**, give it a title and detail, and choose whether it's enabled by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edit">
    Edit any instruction inline to refine its wording.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Toggle">
    Turn instructions on or off. The badge count and every composer reflect the change immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search">
    Filter your instructions by title or detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Delete">
    Remove instructions you no longer need.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You can also manage instructions without leaving the composer: the **Instructions** button opens a quick panel to toggle or create them, with a **Manage all** link to the full page.

## Start from a preset

New to Instructions? The page offers ready-made presets you can use as-is or tweak, including:

- **No em-dashes** — avoid em-dashes; use periods or commas instead
- **Don't sound like AI** — skip formal transitions, hedging, and buzzwords
- **Answer first** — lead with the answer or bottom line
- **Skip the flattery** — be direct and concise
- **My company** / **My role** — give the model standing context about you

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Instructions">
    Go to **Instructions** in the sidebar, or click the **Instructions** button in the composer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Pick a preset or create your own with a clear title and detail. Keep each rule focused on one behavior.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate as usual">
    Head to the Generator or Optimizer. Your enabled instructions are applied automatically — the badge shows how many are active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep instructions short and single-purpose. Several small rules ("Answer first", "No em-dashes") are easier to toggle than one long paragraph.
</Tip>
