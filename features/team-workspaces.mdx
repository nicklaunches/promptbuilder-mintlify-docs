Workspaces keep a **shared prompt library** — with folders and members — separate from your personal one. Everyone has a personal workspace by default; on a paid plan you can create **Team** workspaces and invite people to collaborate.

## Personal vs Team

- **Personal** — your private library, always available. It can't be deleted.
- **Team** — a shared library, folders, and members. Creating one requires a paid plan.

Switch between them with the **workspace switcher** at the top of the sidebar. The active workspace drives your [Prompt Library](/features/prompt-library) — switching reloads My Prompts and folders for that workspace.

## Roles

Every member has one of three roles:

- **Owner** — full control, including billing and deleting the workspace.
- **Admin** — manage members and all prompts and folders.
- **Member** — create and edit prompts and folders.

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Create / edit prompts and folders | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Invite, remove, change member roles | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Rename workspace | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Delete workspace, manage billing | ✓ | — | — |

<Note>
  The owner is protected: they can't be removed or demoted, and only the owner can delete the workspace.
</Note>

## Manage your team

Open **Team members** from the sidebar account menu (or **Manage members** in the workspace switcher) to reach the team settings.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Invite a member">
    Enter their email and pick a role (Member or Admin). They receive an email with an accept link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pending invites">
    See who's been invited and **revoke** an invite before it's accepted. Invites expire after 7 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Change a role or remove">
    Admins can switch a member between Member and Admin, or remove them. Anyone can leave a workspace themselves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Seats">
    A seat counter shows how many of your workspace's seats are in use — members plus pending invites both count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rename or delete">
    Rename the workspace (admins and owner), or delete it (owner only). Deleting removes the shared library, folders, members, and invites.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up a team workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workspace">
    In the sidebar switcher, choose **Create workspace** and give it a name. (Team workspaces require a paid plan.)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Team members**, invite teammates by email, and assign each a role.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build a shared library">
    Save prompts and create folders in the workspace — everyone who joins sees the same library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use folders to keep a shared team library tidy — group prompts by campaign, client, or workflow so teammates find the right one fast.
</Tip>

## Accepting an invite

The invite email links to an accept page. If you're signed in, you join the workspace immediately; if not, you'll be asked to sign in first and then returned to accept. Once you're in, switch to the workspace from the sidebar to see its shared library.
