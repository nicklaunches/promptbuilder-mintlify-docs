Get value fast: generate a prompt, sharpen it, run it, and save it to your Library for reuse.

<Info>
  **Open the app**: https://app.promptbuilder.cc
</Info>

## Meet the composer

Both the **Generator** and **Optimizer** start from the same composer:

- **Prompt type** — what kind of prompt you want (Standard, Research, Writing, Code, and more). Leave it on **Auto** to let Prompt Builder choose.
- **Model** — the AI model you'll run the prompt with. **Universal** works everywhere.
- **Voice** — dictate instead of typing.
- **Attach** — add context files (images, docs, spreadsheets, code).
- **Instructions** — your standing rules, applied to every prompt. The badge shows how many are active.

## Get started in 5 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate your first prompt">
    1. Open **Prompt Generator** in the app.
    2. Describe what you want to accomplish (type, dictate with **Voice**, or **Attach** context).
    3. Pick a **prompt type** (or leave it on Auto) and a **target model**.
    4. Click **Generate**.

    <Tip>
      Stuck on what to write? Click a card under **Prompt suggestions** to drop a starter into the composer.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refine and take the next step">
    Prompt Builder opens the finished prompt in the prompt view. From there you can:
    - **Improve your prompt** — answer a few quick questions to sharpen it
    - **Create follow up prompt** — seed a related prompt from this one
    - Keep refining in chat, then **Save** the version you want to your Library
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optimize an existing prompt">
    1. Open **Prompt Optimizer**.
    2. Paste a prompt you already use (or insert one from your Library).
    3. Click **Optimize**, answer **up to three quick questions**, and get a stronger prompt back.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your Instructions (optional)">
    Open **Instructions** and add standing rules like "Answer first" or "Don't sound like AI" — start from a preset or write your own. They're woven into every prompt automatically; toggle them on or off any time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save, run, and reuse from the Library">
    Open **Prompt Library** to:
    - Find saved prompts in **My Prompts**
    - Filter by category (prompt type), tags, and model
    - Pin your favorites, and **Run** a prompt to try it in a new **Tester** chat
  </Step>
</Steps>
