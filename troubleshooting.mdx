## Common issues

**Tester responses are empty**
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Switch to another tester model and retry.

**Generate or Optimize button is disabled**
- Make sure the input field has content.
- Check for required fields (model selection, topic).

**Prompts are not saving**
- Confirm you are signed in.
- Refresh and retry the Save action.

**"This prompt can't be generated" or "This request was blocked"**
- Our content check blocked the request before it reached a model. No credits were used.
- Rewrite the prompt, attached files or instructions and try again. If you think it was a mistake, email support with what you were trying to do.

**"Our content check is unavailable right now"**
- The check could not run, so nothing was sent to a model and no credits were used. Try again in a few minutes.

## Still stuck?

Email `support@promptbuilder.cc` with steps to reproduce and screenshots.
